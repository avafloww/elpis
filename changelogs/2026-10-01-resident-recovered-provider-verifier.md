# Resident-owned recovered binding verification

Elpis now exposes `elpis.context.verifyRecoveredIsolatedProviderBinding()` only to fresh resident runs carrying process-local origin proof from the exact internal restart-complete or heartbeat producer. Agent publishes that proof only after the complete turn drain contains no person-facing ingress, and `SandboxManager` binds it to the current turn token; matching envelope strings cannot grant access. The read-only check selects the newest historical dark isolated-provider binding, strictly rematerializes it, requires the current boot-resolved target byte-for-byte, and verifies migration, dark-mode, crash-recovery, and zero-effect invariants.

The result is limited to build facts, opaque hashes, and fixed flags. It includes no room, event, branch, profile, request, timestamp, target endpoint, candidate content, or other historical payload, and failures use a generic outward error. Other harness notices (including collaborator/MCP ingress), social, scheduler, watch, worker, direct, restored, inherited, detached, and closed runs are rejected.

This removes the need for a person-authored social marker during routine post-restart acceptance without fabricating ingress or weakening social-world boundaries. Schema v48 adds an immutable monotonic insertion-order ledger for schema-v47 binding receipts and rejects ambiguous upgrades rather than inferring chronology from timestamps. The verifier performs no provider call, network dispatch, tool effect, capsule creation, activation, or continuation change. Deterministic store, presentation/privacy, exact-producer/mixed-turn, and manager-origin regressions cover the path; after deployment, invoke it from the restart-complete internal run and confirm the historical-only receipt.
