# Bind mentions turns to their source channel

Addressed `mentions`-tier turns are now confined to the exact Discord channel or thread that woke the agent. The prior process-local capability named the correct source, but callers omitted it for other targets and the final Discord gate consulted it only when the destination was already send-denied; an otherwise-sendable channel could therefore receive a cross-channel message or typing effect.

The Agent now mints and propagates one nominal capability for every addressed mentions turn, including when the source itself is explicitly sendable, and rejects every mismatched channel, parent, or Console target. A configured harness error notice that would cross the turn boundary is logged locally instead. Discord tracks the sole current capability and requires its exact identity before every message chunk and repeating typing effect, rejecting omitted, forged, stale, wrong-guild, and wrong-channel credentials. Each send also pins its entry authorization and issuance generation before speech capture or channel lookup, so expiry or an intervening mention scope cannot downgrade a pending operation into an ordinary send. Sandbox runs capture an opaque outbound origin plus the exact Agent-turn identity and source channel before execution and retain them across detachment. Explicit send and typing calls, and the automatic typing pause/resume around `elpis.sleep`, therefore cannot discard a stale mention capability, retarget another channel, alter a later turn's sleep depth, or inherit a newer authorization. If a mention origin cannot produce a valid transport capability, including when issuance throws, its run captures a permanently denied origin rather than the explicit unscoped marker used by direct and social runs. Direct and social turns resume normally after scope expiry, while guild, explicit-channel, mute, and deafen denials still dominate.

Regression tests cover omitted and forged credentials against an otherwise-sendable destination, an omitted-token fetch race across scope expiry, a complete issue-and-expire scope change during an unscoped fetch, detached send and typing continuations, missing-issuer continuations after both turn finish and context clear, throwing and invalid-current issuers, sleep-resume scope propagation, an immediately queued same-channel generation, stale exact-source attachment and speech attempts, explicit unscoped restoration, thread-versus-parent identity, Console confinement, token expiry between message chunks and before an internal turn queued at the wake-arm boundary begins, repeating typing, and an explicitly sendable mentions source. Focused tests, the deterministic suite, build, and release checks were run before publication. After deployment, verify a real mention can answer in its source room while an attempted cross-channel send is refused.
