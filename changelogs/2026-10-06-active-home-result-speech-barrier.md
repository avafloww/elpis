# Active home result speech barrier

Schema v58 adds a separate active-home speech-attempt barrier instead of widening the historical home-text delivery tables. One issued active provider success, its observed provider effect, one canonical speech attempt, and one exact private result capsule now commit atomically. A deferred obligation prevents success rows from landing without the barrier, and migration rejects any preexisting active success rather than turning it into delayed-send authority.

The capsule cannot be shared. No speech effect, Discord request, receipt, continuation advance, Agent routing, deployment, or graph activation is added by this change; successful branches remain running and cannot recover or transition until the later delivery stage is implemented.
