# Bind the first real routed world profile

Schema 46 adds a resident-only `elpis.context.bindCurrentWorldProfile(derivationId)` action. A live resident run can bind the accepted contract and identity derivation to the exact wake-defining Discord or Signal ingress lineage already recorded by shadow mode; callers cannot supply or retarget the world, event, profile, or head.

The operation stays dark and non-runnable. One transaction creates only a contract-and-identity system profile, its revision-one world profile head, and an immutable binding receipt. It creates no branch, provider request, effect authority, activation, or continuation advance, and formatting or secret-redaction failure rolls the transaction back.

After deployment, enable `context_graph.shadow_enabled`, receive a genuine social ingress, invoke the action from that live run, and verify the stored receipt plus unchanged dark activation state before continuing graph work.
